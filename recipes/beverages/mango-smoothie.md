# Mango Smoothie

A creamy, tropical mango smoothie — refreshing, naturally sweet, and ready in minutes.

## Details

| | |
|---|---|
| **Prep Time** | 5 minutes |
| **Cook Time** | 0 minutes |
| **Total Time** | 5 minutes |
| **Servings** | 2 |
| **Difficulty** | Easy |

## Ingredients

- 2 cups frozen mango chunks
- 1 banana
- 1 cup plain yogurt
- 1/2 cup orange juice
- 1/2 cup milk (dairy or plant-based)
- 1 tbsp honey (optional)

## Instructions

1. Add the frozen mango chunks, banana, yogurt, orange juice, and milk to a blender.
2. Blend on high until smooth and creamy, about 1–2 minutes.
3. Taste and add honey if you'd like extra sweetness.
4. Pour into glasses and serve immediately.

## Notes

- Use fresh mango with a handful of ice cubes if frozen mango is unavailable.
- Swap yogurt for coconut yogurt to make it dairy-free.
- Add a handful of spinach for a green smoothie variation — you won't taste it!
