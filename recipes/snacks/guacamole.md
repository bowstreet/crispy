# Guacamole

Fresh, zesty guacamole made with ripe avocados — perfect for dipping chips or topping your favorite dishes.

## Details

| | |
|---|---|
| **Prep Time** | 10 minutes |
| **Cook Time** | 0 minutes |
| **Total Time** | 10 minutes |
| **Servings** | 4 |
| **Difficulty** | Easy |

## Ingredients

- 3 ripe avocados
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup diced onion
- 3 tbsp chopped fresh cilantro
- 2 roma tomatoes, diced
- 1 tsp minced garlic
- 1 pinch ground cayenne pepper

## Instructions

1. Cut the avocados in half, remove the pits, and scoop the flesh into a mixing bowl.
2. Mash the avocado with a fork to your desired consistency (chunky or smooth).
3. Stir in the lime juice and salt.
4. Mix in the onion, cilantro, tomatoes, and garlic.
5. Add cayenne pepper and adjust seasoning to taste.
6. Serve immediately with tortilla chips.

## Notes

- For best results, use avocados that yield slightly to gentle pressure.
- To prevent browning, press plastic wrap directly onto the surface before refrigerating.
- Add diced jalapeño for extra heat.
