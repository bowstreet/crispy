# Caesar Salad Wrap

A portable twist on the classic Caesar salad — crisp romaine, parmesan, and creamy dressing wrapped in a warm tortilla.

## Details

| | |
|---|---|
| **Prep Time** | 10 minutes |
| **Cook Time** | 5 minutes |
| **Total Time** | 15 minutes |
| **Servings** | 2 |
| **Difficulty** | Easy |

## Ingredients

- 2 large flour tortillas
- 2 cups chopped romaine lettuce
- 1/2 cup shredded parmesan cheese
- 1/2 cup croutons
- 1/4 cup Caesar dressing
- 1 grilled chicken breast, sliced (optional)
- Freshly ground black pepper

## Instructions

1. Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet over medium heat for about 30 seconds per side.
2. In a bowl, toss the romaine lettuce, parmesan, and croutons with the Caesar dressing.
3. If using chicken, add the sliced grilled chicken to the salad mixture.
4. Divide the mixture evenly between the two tortillas.
5. Fold in the sides of each tortilla and roll up tightly.
6. Slice in half diagonally and serve immediately.

## Notes

- For a lighter version, use a yogurt-based Caesar dressing.
- Add cherry tomatoes or avocado for extra flavor.
- Works great as a make-ahead lunch — just keep the dressing on the side until ready to eat.
