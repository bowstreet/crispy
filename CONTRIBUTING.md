# Contributing to Crispy Recipes

Thank you for your interest in contributing a recipe! Here's how to get started.

## How to Add a Recipe

1. **Fork** this repository.
2. **Copy** the [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) file.
3. **Rename** your copy to a descriptive kebab-case filename (e.g., `classic-pancakes.md`).
4. **Fill in** your recipe details following the template format.
5. **Place** your recipe file in the appropriate category folder under `recipes/`.
6. **Submit** a pull request with your new recipe.

## Guidelines

- **One recipe per file.** Keep each recipe in its own Markdown file.
- **Use the template.** Follow the format in [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) for consistency.
- **Choose the right category.** Place your recipe in the most fitting folder:
  - `recipes/breakfast/`
  - `recipes/lunch/`
  - `recipes/dinner/`
  - `recipes/desserts/`
  - `recipes/snacks/`
  - `recipes/beverages/`
- **Use clear, descriptive filenames.** Use lowercase kebab-case (e.g., `chicken-tikka-masala.md`).
- **Be specific with measurements.** Use standard units (cups, tbsp, tsp, oz, g, ml, etc.).
- **Write clear instructions.** Number each step and keep them concise.
- **Credit your sources.** If a recipe is adapted from another source, mention it in the Notes section.

## Code of Conduct

Be respectful and welcoming. We're here to share great food with each other!
